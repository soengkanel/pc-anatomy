Case Study · Stuxnet (the perfect attack)

A ~500 KB worm, built by two governments, slipped into a nuclear facility that had NO internet — and physically destroyed its machines. The most well-engineered cyberattack ever found. Imagine it hitting a Cambodian factory or power station — same chips, same risk.
